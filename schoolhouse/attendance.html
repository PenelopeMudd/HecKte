Attendance

Attendance is the systemic requirement. Absenteeism is the absence of that quality. The lack belongs to the requirement, not to the child — and the school requires something it cannot produce. Nine factors, ordered by what a school can actually do.

The school cannot move the child’s house. But it can redesign the friction between the house and the classroom.

We don’t own the geography. We do own part of the journey.

Barriers — whether attendance is possible

You connect; you don’t fix. Agency sits with the household at these bands.

Factor 1

Physical Health

Is the body able to be here today — and what happens when it wasn’t? The school owns the return, not the illness.

Factor 2

Transportation & Access

Can the student physically get here? The room’s metaphor made literal — the strongest Redesign column of the barriers.

Factor 3

Basic Needs & Economic Stability

Is the material floor under this student steady enough for a school day to sit on top of it? The largest factor; the thinnest Redesign column.

Factor 4

Family Capacity & Caregiving

Does the household have the bandwidth to execute a school morning? The agreement is with an adult the school may never have met.

Factor 5 — the hinge

Mental & Behavioral Health

Can the student tolerate being here? Where barrier turns into experience, and where the building itself is a variable.

Reasons — whether attendance is chosen

This is your zone. Agency moves toward the student across these bands.

Factor 6

School Climate & Safety

What is it like to be here, and does being here cost anything? The first factor whose answer is authored almost entirely inside the walls.

Factor 7

Relationships & Belonging

Is there anyone here who would notice they were gone? The lever — the factor a school-based relationship-holder can actually move.

Factor 8

Academic Experience

Is the work survivable, and is it worth doing? The factor that compounds: absence makes work harder; harder work makes returning more expensive.

Factor 9

Meaning, Identity & Value

Why is this school worth attending? This room’s Missing Measure — least studied in the literature, most within school design.
