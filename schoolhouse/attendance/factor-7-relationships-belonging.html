Relationships & Belonging

Is there anyone here who would notice they were gone? Of the nine factors, this is the one a school-based relationship-holder can actually move — and the one that buffers the others.

The participation question

Would being missed be true? Not does the student like school. Whether their absence registers as a loss to a specific person, and whether they know it would.

What you can do here — and what you can’t

Attendance is a requirement the school cannot produce. No one in the building can make a student come. What the school can do is redesign the friction between the house and the classroom. This factor is the largest piece of that you personally hold.

ObserveWho has no one · who arrives and speaks to nobody · who is absent without anyone remarking on it
InfluenceWhether a specific adult knows a specific student · whether the student knows that adult noticed · whether return after absence is welcomed or interrogated
RedesignAdvisory structures · who greets whom · what happens on the first day back · which adult owns which students by name

You cannot produce attendance. A student’s absence is not your failure.

What you can do is make sure that when they are gone, someone notices, and when they come back, it costs them nothing.

That is a smaller claim than most attendance programs make, and it is one you can actually keep.

Read through Restorative Practices

(This room’s lens. See Restorative Practices — Basics.)

Absence is a lapsed agreement, not a violation. The restorative question is what happened, not why weren’t you here — the second invites a defense. A student who returns to a demand for justification learns that coming back costs something.

Relationship is the mechanism, not the sentiment. It’s the channel a return travels on. A student with no one here has nothing to come back to, only somewhere to be required.

By grade band

The relationship that matters is with the household. A caregiver who has one warm, non-summoning contact at the school is more likely to get a child there than one whose only contact is a letter about absences.

Both, and the child begins to have preferences. Roles in the room’s operation start to matter — a student who runs something is expected in a way attendance records don’t capture.

The handoff. The move is making return cheap. Someone who says good to see you and nothing else. A way back in that doesn’t require accounting for where you were.

The student decides, and the reasons change kind. One adult who knows her situation and doesn’t moralize about it is the version that works here. The bar is that the school contains at least one person for whom her absence is a specific loss.

Evidence

Connecticut — LEAP home-visiting program + restorative practices in statute. Chronic absenteeism fell 20% → 17.7% in 2023–24. A working example, not a controlled demonstration.

Klevan (2021) — whole-school restorative practice associated with improved climate and connectedness and reduced exclusionary discipline. Demonstrated as association.

Belonging conditions motivation — students engage where they feel accepted; belonging is a precondition, not a reward. Demonstrated as a mechanism.

Honest limit: no study isolates “a student had one trusted adult” as a cause of attendance. Treat this as well-supported and not proven.
