Relationships & Belonging

Is there anyone here who would notice they were gone? The lever — of the nine factors, this is the one a school-based relationship-holder can actually move.

What is it

Whether a specific person in this building holds a specific student’s absence as a specific loss — not a data point, a loss — and whether the student knows it.

This is the lever. Of the nine factors, this is the one a school-based relationship-holder can actually move. A student can face poverty, illness, anxiety, and a school with a bad climate and still come, if someone here knows them and makes return cheap.

The return is the mechanism. A student who comes back to good to see you and nothing else has learned that coming back costs nothing.

What did we learn

How big it is. Belonging conditions motivation — students engage where they feel accepted, and belonging functions as a precondition, not a reward. (Deans for Impact, The Science of Learning, 2nd ed.) Demonstrated. Exclusionary discipline increases future misbehavior and dropout risk. (Klevan, 2021.) Demonstrated.

What actually returns days. Connecticut’s LEAP — relationship-building home visits in the districts with the highest chronic absenteeism, running since 2021, alongside restorative practice written into statute (mandatory July 2025). Chronic absenteeism fell 20% → 17.7% in 2023–24, with the greatest gains in the early grades. Working example — not a controlled demonstration.

What making return cheap does. The restorative question is what happened, not why weren’t you here — the second invites a defense. A student who returns to a demand for justification learns that coming back costs something. Absence generates its own next absence, and it does so through the return, not through the days themselves. Reasoned — structural observation from the convergent discipline analysis.

Honest limit: nothing in our sources isolates “a student had one trusted adult” as a cause of attendance. What exists is consistent association across climate, connectedness, and attendance, plus one state-scale working example. Treat this as well-supported and not proven.

What do we know so far

Absence is a lapsed agreement, not a violation. The school requires attendance and can only obtain it by consent. A student who stops coming has withdrawn from an agreement — sometimes because the conditions collapsed, sometimes deliberately. Either reading calls for repair, not enforcement.

The reachable person changes by band.

K–2

The relationship that matters is with the household. A six-year-old has no say in whether they arrive. A caregiver who has one warm, non-summoning contact at the school is more likely to get a child there than one whose only contact has been a letter about absences. Connecticut’s greatest gains were in kindergarten and the early grades — the bands where household agency is total.

3–5

Both, and the child begins to have preferences. The relationship with the household still executes arrival; the relationship with the child determines whether they want to be delivered. Roles in the room’s operation start to matter — a student who runs something is expected in a way the attendance record does not capture.

6–8

The student can now decline. Social reasons become primary: who they would face, what they would have to explain about being gone. The move is making return cheap — someone who says good to see you and nothing else, before asking anything.

9–12

A job, siblings to watch, a calculation that the day isn’t worth what it costs. One adult who knows her situation and does not moralize about it is the version that works here. The bar is that this building contains at least one person for whom her absence is a specific loss.

Emerging questions

None yet.

The boundary

You cannot make a student feel belonging by announcing that she belongs here. The relationship is built contact by contact, and it belongs to a specific person who knows a specific student — not to the institution.

What you own is whether this building contains such a person for her. Whether the return costs something. Whether good to see you is the first thing said, before anything else.

The lever is the cheapest move in this room. It does not require a program or a budget. It requires a person paying attention to a specific absence.
