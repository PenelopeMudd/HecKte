Physical Space

A standing decision that executes constantly — and the one instructional variable that teachers are least trained to see.

Teachers are trained to think about instruction, not environment. But the room is a standing decision that runs all day, every day, without anyone deciding again — which makes it higher leverage than almost any individual instructional choice, and systematically undertended. Where materials live, what the walls say, whether a circle is physically possible: these are permanent conditions, and the friction or support they provide is invisible until you look for it.

There is no research finding that one seating configuration outperforms another across contexts. The finding is narrower and more useful: a space organized around the work that’s actually happening reduces the transaction costs of doing that work. The question is always what you’re trying to make easy.

What has to be in place

Nothing — this is a foundation.
