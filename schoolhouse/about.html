The researcher

Kim Starkey, Ph.D.

The researcher, Kim Starkey, Ph.D., who despises referring to herself in the third person but respects the convention, is a translational educational researcher who works at the intersection of learning science, restorative practice, human-centered design, and systems thinking. Her work focuses on helping educators make research more usable—and helping researchers better understand the realities of classrooms.

Her writing is at An Unreliable Translator.
