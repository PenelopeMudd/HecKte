Science of Learning — supporting nodes

Cognitive Load

The mind's workspace is limited; extraneous load crowds out the germane load that is the actual learning. Linked from: transitions, routines, expectations & norms.

Working Memory

The small, active store where thinking happens. Automaticity from rehearsed routines frees it for complex work. Linked from: routines, visual supports, expectations & norms.

Attention

Finite and easily captured by whatever is most salient. Protected by clean transitions and low load. Linked from: transitions, engagement.
